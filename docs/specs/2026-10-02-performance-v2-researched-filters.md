# Performance v2: researched selection filters

## Goal and scope

Port the accepted `Output/FilterExp/_tools` selection rules into the Panel's existing finalist pipeline. Existing fixed stages 1–5 retain their behavior. The new fixed stages are 6 `pair_side_pnl_upper_half`, 7 `structural_stage_1`, 8 `structural_stage_2`, and 9 `pair_side_stage_3`. Older movable stages follow stage 9 in their present relative order and are disabled by default. Each stage reads only current survivors; exclusions are monotone. The existing selection snapshot, stage flags, reasons and XLSX remain the audit surface. This work does not run a tester or turn source metrics into tested MRS3 results.

## Inputs and stage contracts

Use current ACTIVE Performance v2 candidates and raw finite decimal metrics. Each run uses one frozen selection config. Group keys are pair and side; stages 7–8 also use TF, and stage 7 uses ORD. Display rounding never affects decisions. Missing/nonfinite metrics do not create adverse evidence. An invalid structural core row is retained but cannot replace another row. Stage 7–8 pass 4ORD through; stage 9 covers ORD 1–4. Preserve the research rules' deterministic replacement and tie logic; replacement IDs identify the strategy that directly won the comparison, even if a later stage excludes that strategy.

Stage 6: on the incoming survivor snapshot, independently for full `dd5_proxy` and B `ab_return_b_30d_pct`, sort the finite values descending, take the first `(n+1)//2`, and use their median as reference. Only a positive reference creates a threshold, respectively 50% and 60% of that reference. Exclude only a finite row value strictly below its metric threshold. Both distributions come from the same incoming snapshot. Missing values are absent from the distribution and pass their gate; finite zero and negatives remain valid inputs. A nonpositive reference makes that metric gate unavailable, as in the research oracle.

Stages 7–8: the current row can be replaced by an eligible same/higher Shift (stage 7 same ORD, TF; stage 8 same TF, candidate ORD>= and Shift>= with at least one strict) only if it has no material core advantage or Hold protection against that replacement. B and DD5 are higher-is-better; DD is lower-is-better. Material differences are strict `> max(absolute, relative * max(abs(a), abs(b)))`: B `(2, .25)`, DD5 `(3, .25)`, DD `(1, .25)`. Points advantage compares arithmetic means by a strict `> .40 * max(means)`; stage 7 additionally requires every aligned current order to have at least 40% of candidate points. Stage 8 uses a 30% weakest-order floor for cross-ORD comparison and requires a 1ORD current plateau to be at least 110% of a multi-ORD replacement's strongest plateau for Points protection. Open MA uses mean difference strictly `>2.6`; Close MA difference is `>=3`. Hold p95 protection is current duration shorter than replacement by strictly more than 20% of replacement; median by strictly more than 30%, unless current p95 is strictly more than 15% worse than replacement p95. The exact candidate graph, retained selection and tie behavior are defined by `performance_v2_rules.py` and `structural_tolerances.py` in the research tree.

Stage 9: across all TF/ORD within pair+side, a retained candidate must be raw nonworse in B and DD5 (`>=`) and DD (`<=`), and materially better in at least two with the same B/DD5/DD limits. The current row survives a candidate when it has Points mean advantage `>40%` with a weakest-order floor of 30%, plus the 1ORD strongest-plateau 110% rule where applicable; or qualifying Hold p95/median protection with the 15% p95 veto. Candidate order and replacement tie order follow `pair_side_stage3.py`.

Compare Points and Open MA means by exact cross-multiplication before applying their strict 40% and 2.6 boundaries. Equality is not an advantage. A same-stage replacement may be retained through a Hold rescue; its final decision is not necessarily named `KEEP`.

## Configuration and UI

Blank Settings fields are omitted from saves. To restore a default, enter its shown default value.

Put the listed ratios and limits in `config.performance.json` under the existing `finalist_selection` section, with Decimal validation and defaults for older configs. Expose key limits on a dedicated Performance v2 section of the Panel Settings screen. A local settings API accepts allowlisted edits with an expected config digest, rejects invalid/stale edits without mutation, preserves unknown existing keys, and writes atomically. The four new filters show short explanations below their names in the selection panel. Existing stage IDs remain stable. Filter order is fixed through stage 9; later stages are user movable and can be explicitly enabled. The Panel XLSX keeps its existing sheets and columns: for an excluded row, put the stage ID and short reason in the existing `Причина` column and the direct comparison winner strategy ID in the existing `Auto Analog Of ID` column. Do not add research-stage status/reason/replacement columns or Excel-style `DROP`/`KEEP` flags.

## Acceptance evidence

Focused tests cover fixed-stage regression, upper-half odd/even median and independent populations, exact boundaries, missing values, ORD4 pass-through, structural Points/MA/Hold protection, same/higher Shift eligibility, cross-ORD comparison, two-of-three plus raw nonworse, deterministic replacement, config validation and save conflict, UI order/descriptions and legacy defaults. Execute tests from `.venv` with C: temporary storage, review the scoped diff independently, and preserve unrelated dirty worktree files.
